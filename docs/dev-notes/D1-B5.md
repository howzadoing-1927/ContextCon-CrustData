- Ticket: B5. Branch: `feat/backend-core`. Base: `c89a498` (B4).
- Migration `0003_b5_ledger_order`: `decisions.created_at` defaults to `clock_timestamp()`, so entries written in one transaction stay chronological. Applied to Supabase.
- Contracts: `DraftProposal` and `VerificationResult` are added to `model_io.py` (`extra=forbid`, so no recipient, account or calendar fields are possible). No API shape changes. `MissionDetail` now fills `prospects[].criteria`, `fit` and `hypotheses`, plus `conversation` (state `drafting`).

## What was built
- **`services/core/qualification.py`:** deterministic eligibility. It requires identity supported AND every must-have passing AND no exclusion matched AND no conflict. Unknown is never pass. Criteria are identity, geography, industry (weak speciality allowed only with observed evidence), must-have rules (funding round ladder, headcount; unknown fields are `unknown`), exclusions and `no_conflict`.
- **`services/models/draft.py`:** the drafter (fast deployment) and verifier (decision deployment) use the doc 04 prompts verbatim (`draft.v1`, `verify.v1`). Envelope `drafting_rules` and verifier `policy_notes` add precision without altering the doc prompts.
- **Deterministic draft checks:**
  - Claims must be exact substrings of the body. Offsets are recomputed only for a unique exact match.
  - Evidence must come from the supplied set, and seller facts must cite approved fact IDs.
  - Hypotheses must be worded as possibilities.
  - No slots, emails, URLs, header lines or completion claims; no forbidden promises; the mandated simulation sentence must be present.
  - Repairs (fact IDs misplaced into `evidence_ids`, offsets, the simulation sentence listed as a claim) are recorded, never silent.
  - One revision at most, then `needs_human`.
- **`services/core/policy_gate.py`:** doc 04 order. Steps 1–6 are evaluated, and every check is recorded including passes. Steps 7–8 (budget and token checks, immutable action and lock recheck) are recorded as `deferred` to dispatch (B6) and never as passed. The recipient comes from the launch snapshot, never from model output.
- **`services/worker/qualify.py`:** `qualify_and_draft` writes fit, the ledger, usage, the conversation and the next task in one fenced transaction. On success: campaign `stage=send`, conversation `drafting`, and a `send_email` task queued with **no handler** (nothing sends until B6).

## Ledger kinds written per run
`campaign_launch`, `normalize_mission` (the research plan), `research`, then `qualify` (one per prospect), `select_prospect`, `draft` (one per attempt), `verify` (one per attempt), and `policy_gate`.

- Request: "Series B–D software development companies in India, VP of Sales".
- Fits: 3 `qualified` and 2 `needs_evidence` (identity, industry and funding unknown, so blocked).
- Selected: a Series C Indian software company with a current "Vice President of Sales".
- Draft round 1 was rejected by the deterministic checks (claim text not verbatim; the verifier had passed it). Round 2 was accepted, the verifier passed it (gpt-4.1), and the gate passed steps 1–6.
- Usage: 5 model calls, 10,443 tokens in, 1,033 out; 9 research calls.
- Tasks: `normalize_mission`, `research` and `qualify_and_draft` succeeded; `send_email` is ready (inert).
- Earlier live attempts exposed three issues, all fixed with tests:
  - The verifier cited a fact ID as an evidence UUID, which crashed the ledger write. Non-UUID references are now kept separately.
  - `DecisionView` rejected the gate's extra check fields, which crashed the view. They are now folded into `name` and `reason`.
  - The drafter paraphrased claims and planner assumptions leaked into the draft; tightened through drafting rules.

## Tests (local throwaway Postgres; full Supabase suite intentionally skipped for speed)
- `test_qualify_draft.py`, 18 tests:
  - All criteria pass gives qualified. **An unknown must-have gives needs_evidence.** A failing must-have or exclusion gives rejected. Conflict and unsupported identity are ineligible.
  - **Unsupported claims are flagged** (seller claim without an approved fact, evidence outside the envelope, unapproved fact ID, claim text missing from the body).
  - Unsafe content: email, URL, header, completion claim, forbidden promise.
  - Offset and fact-ID repairs. The schema rejects `to_email`.
  - Handler: happy path (ledger order, deferred steps 7–8, conversation, inert send task), one revision then pass, two failed verifications lead to `needs_human`, a suppressed company is blocked at the gate, and an unknown must-have means `no_match` with no model call.
- Full local suite: 218 passed, 5 skipped.

## Limitations
- Qualification only knows criteria that map to Crustdata data (geography, industry, funding, headcount). Any other must-have is `unknown`, so it is ineligible by design.
- Exclusions are matched on name, industry and speciality text with a simple stem. Business-model exclusions (for example "no agencies") need richer evidence.
- Prospect choice is "first eligible". There is no ranking.
- Drafting relies on rules in the envelope; the model still fails the first round often (1 of 2 rounds in the live run). Each failure is visible in the ledger.
- The gate's steps 7–8 and the actual send are B6. The seller connection is still the seeded, dev-only row, so B6 needs D2's real OAuth connection.
