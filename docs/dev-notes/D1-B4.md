## Live receipt (Supabase + Azure + Crustdata, 2026-11-06 ~02:25 IST; IDs truncated)
- Request: "Series B to Series D software development companies in India and their VP of Sales".
- Normalized by Azure gpt-4.1-mini (1 call, 1,272 tokens in, 164 out).
- **Mapping:**
  - India mapped to `locations.country`.
  - "software development" mapped to `basic_info.industries = Software Development`.
  - The funding rule mapped to `funding.last_round_type`.
  - "VP of Sales" mapped to title variants `[VP, Sales]` and `[Vice, President, Sales]`.
  - The model's 2 assumptions were left unmapped, for qualification.
- **Calls:**
  - 4 free autocomplete calls.
  - Company search: 10 results, 2.30 credits, `total_count` 28.
  - Person search ×4: 1, 0, 2 and 1 results, 0.92 credits in total.
  - Company enrich ×3: 6.00 credits.
  - Totals: 8 billable calls of a 10-call budget, **9.22 credits**.
- **Outcome:**
  - 3 of 4 examined companies had a current VP Sales whose employer ID matched; those 3 were enriched. The 4th is `needs_evidence` (no matching current employee).
  - **71 evidence rows**, all `observed`, with no email-like values.
  - Decision accepted; the campaign moved to `stage=qualify` (version 3); `qualify_and_draft` is `ready`.
  - Sample evidence: `crustdata:/person/search#experience.employment_details.current[…].title` = "VP Sales and Business Development", sha256 `1aea32df7a2b…`, request `aa06d2e1…`, 0.23 credits.
  - The temporary organization and user were purged.
- **Bug found by this receipt, fixed after it:** the model expressed "Series B to D" as a range of `["Series B","Series D"]`, and the mapper searched only B and D. Range rules now expand along the round ladder (b, c, d), with a test and a free live autocomplete check. The receipt above predates the fix.

## Limitations
- Discovery is the first 10 matches sorted by `crustdata_company_id`, not a relevance ranking. Small companies often have no exact title match, so they become `needs_evidence`.
- The speciality fallback (for example "fintech") relies on companies' own tags. It is reported as `weak_matches` for B5.
- Exclusions, non-must-have rules and assumptions are not search filters. B5 must apply them.
- No `source_as_of`: per-field update timestamps are not requested.
- Credits are stored in decision metadata, not in `Usage.estimated_cost`, because credits are not a currency.
- Rate pacing is per process; one live worker in P0.
