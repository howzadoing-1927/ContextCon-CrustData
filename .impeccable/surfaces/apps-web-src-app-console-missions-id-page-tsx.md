# Mission detail

Mode: Operate. Primary surface of the product; mission list, new mission, setup and sign-in inherit this world.

Audience: seller/operator and exception owner; evaluators watching the screen.
Job: see what the worker is doing, waiting on or blocked by, and verify each decision against its evidence and provider receipts.
Constraints: see PRODUCT.md capabilities and constraints; mode badge always visible; provenance per step.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Proof is the interface. The screen refuses the category default of a chat transcript with a progress bar; it is a working case file where every state, claim and effect carries its source and receipt.

OWN-WORLD: Ink-slate ground tinted toward blue, a cooler shell layer, hairline separation, one warm amber signal for attention and the current station, square provenance stamps (LIVE solid green, CACHED blue outline, FIXTURE violet hatched, SIMULATED BUYER cyan with mask glyph), IBM Plex Sans with Plex Mono reserved for machine data.

STORY: The operator sees the stage and what happens next, understands why this prospect was chosen, sees what was sent and what the buyer actually said, and trusts the booking because its event ID and checks are on screen.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Top bar with mission request, status chip, version, usage and Pause/Stop on the right. Left 220 px: the mission route. Centre: prospect case, then conversation. Right 380 px: decision ledger with a sticky header. Exceptions appear as an amber strip under the top bar that opens the drawer.

FORM: User-pinned "operator console, dark" direction; roll not run. seed: none (unavailable)

Signature interaction: the mission route advances station by station as polled state changes; terminal outcomes branch off as a spur at the station where they happened.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
